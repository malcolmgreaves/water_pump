GOAL:
Ensure a controlled and reliable transfer of water from a large reservoir into a coffee machine’s internal reservoir.
::END GOAL::

DESIGN:

COMPONENTS:

Large Reservoir – Holds the main water supply.
Pump – Moves water from the large reservoir to the coffee machine.
Flow Sensor – Monitors the amount of water transferred.
Solenoid Valve – Controls water flow into the coffee machine.
Controller (CONTROLLER) – Manages pump activation, monitors flow, and regulates filling.
Water Level Sensor (in coffee machine’s reservoir) – Detects when the internal reservoir is full.
Tubing & Connectors – Facilitates water transport between components.
::END COMPONENTS::

CONNECTIONS:

Large Reservoir → Pump (Suction line to draw water from the reservoir).
Pump → Flow Sensor (Pressurized water is pushed through the flow sensor).
Flow Sensor → Solenoid Valve (Flow data is sent to the controller to manage the solenoid).
Solenoid Valve → Coffee Machine Reservoir (Controls water entry into the coffee machine).
Water Level Sensor → Controller (Sends signals indicating water level in the coffee machine).
Controller → Pump (Activates/deactivates the pump based on sensor inputs).
Controller → Solenoid Valve (Opens/closes the valve based on flow and level conditions).
::END CONNECTIONS::

SYSTEM OPERATION:
The system begins operation when the coffee machine’s internal reservoir requires water. The water level sensor detects when water is low and signals the controller. The controller then activates the pump to draw water from the large reservoir. As the pump runs, water passes through the flow sensor, which provides feedback to the controller to monitor the transfer. The solenoid valve remains open, allowing water to flow into the coffee machine.

Once the internal reservoir reaches the desired water level, the level sensor sends a signal to the controller, which then shuts off the pump and closes the solenoid valve, stopping further flow. This process ensures that the coffee machine is automatically refilled without overfilling or requiring manual intervention.

::END SYSTEM OPERATION::

Now evaluating the design:

FEASIBILITY:
The design is feasible and can be implemented using commercially available components. The controller can effectively regulate water flow using input from the sensors. Proper tubing and pressure management will be necessary to ensure consistent operation.
::END FEASIBILITY::

ADJUSTMENTS:

Power Source Consideration: Ensure the pump, controller, and solenoid valve are powered adequately. If the coffee machine has a power interface, integration may be required.
Pressure Management: If the pump generates excessive pressure, a pressure regulator might be needed to prevent damage.
Backflow Prevention: A check valve should be included to prevent water from flowing back into the large reservoir.
Compatibility with Coffee Machine: Ensure the coffee machine’s reservoir can receive external water input without triggering malfunctions.
::END ADJUSTMENTS::

PRODUCTS:

Pump: ULKA EAP5 Vibratory Pump (commonly used in coffee machines).
Flow Sensor: DIGITEN G1/4" Flow Meter Water Flow Sensor.
Solenoid Valve: U.S. Solid 12V DC Normally Closed Solenoid Valve.
Controller: Arduino or ESP32 microcontroller for automation.
Water Level Sensor: XKC-Y25-T12V Non-Contact Water Level Sensor.
Tubing & Connectors: Food-grade silicone tubing with appropriate fittings.
::END PRODUCTS::

FINAL SUGGESTIONS:

Fail-Safe Features: Implement a timer-based shutoff to prevent continuous pumping in case of sensor failure.
Leak Detection: Consider adding a water leak sensor to prevent spills.
Integration with Coffee Machine: If the coffee machine has a built-in water detection system, it should be interfaced properly to avoid conflicting inputs.
Wireless Monitoring: If desired, an IoT module could provide remote monitoring of water levels and pump operation.
::END FINAL SUGGESTIONS::
