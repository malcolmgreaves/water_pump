FEASIBILITY:
The design is overall feasible. It includes essential components—a controller, sensors, pump, and logging with error handling—to manage the autonomous water transfer. However, additional considerations regarding waterproofing and physical safety mechanisms should be addressed to ensure long-term reliable operation.

::END FEASIBILITY

ADJUSTMENTS:

Upgrade waterproofing for cables and connections by using sealed connectors or waterproof enclosures instead of relying solely on waterproof tape.

Add a check valve in the food-grade tubing to prevent backflow from the coffee machine’s internal reservoir into the large reservoir.

Use industrial-grade mounting hardware for securing the computer and sensors to the reservoirs, ensuring stable long-term operation.

Verify that the power supplies for both the controller and pump are rated for continuous operation over a 10-hour period and include surge or voltage regulation if needed.

Consider incorporating a secondary, mechanical safety mechanism (e.g., a float switch) in the coffee machine’s reservoir to act as a backup if the sensors or controller fail.
::END ADJUSTMENTS

::END PRODUCTS

FINAL SUGGESTIONS:

Prototype and simulate the system operation for extended periods to ensure reliability, particularly under continuous operation for 10 hours.

Test the email notification system thoroughly to ensure prompt alerts during WARN or FATAL events.

Consider integrating remote monitoring (e.g., a cloud-based dashboard) for proactive maintenance and troubleshooting.

Evaluate environmental factors (temperature, humidity, movement of reservoirs) to further refine sensor placement and wiring security.
